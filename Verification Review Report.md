Decision Rationale

The manuscript has matured substantially from Round 1. Every major intellectual concern — the diagnostic triad recalibration, pathological description consistency, DRE documentation, MRI justification, hematuria resolution, renal function reporting, lymphadenopathy attribution — has been addressed with well-crafted, clinically honest prose. The paper is now publication-quality in its argumentation and clinical completeness.

Two issues prevent immediate acceptance:

Figure 1 is still absent. This is a genuine showstopper — not an editorial technicality. Histopathology is the definitive evidence for this case. A journal cannot publish "pathological diagnosis confirmed epidermoid cyst" without the histological image. Once the H&E image is obtained from the pathology department and de-identified (300 dpi minimum), this issue is resolved in a single action.

Citation style must be converted to Vancouver. JFMA, like virtually all Elsevier clinical journals, requires numbered Vancouver references. The current APA author-date format will trigger automatic rejection at format checking. This is a mechanical conversion (the content of the references is correct) but it must be done before upload.

For JFMA specifically, this case is an excellent fit: Taiwan-based surgical case, rare presentation with strong CT mimicry, bilingual abstract, complete mandatory disclosures. The word count is at the JFMA ceiling — manage the headroom carefully.
