We are pleased to submit our manuscript entitled "A Large Epidermoid
Cyst of the Pelvic Floor Mimicking Rectal Malignancy: A Case Report"
(中文標題：骨盆底巨大硬皮囊腫擬似直腸惡性腫瘤：病例報告) for
consideration as a Case Report in the Fu-Jen Journal of Medicine.

This report describes a 61-year-old male whose 10 cm pelvic floor
epidermoid cyst was initially misidentified as a rectal submucosal
malignancy with metastatic lymphadenopathy on contrast-enhanced CT.
We highlight a diagnostic triad — negative colonoscopy, normal tumor
markers, and absent mass enhancement — that redirected management away
from an oncologic work-up toward prophylactic ureteral stenting and
complete surgical excision, with an excellent outcome. We believe this
case offers a practical diagnostic framework that may help other
clinicians avoid unnecessary oncologic work-up when confronted with a
similarly mimicking pelvic mass.
